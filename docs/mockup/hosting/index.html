Hosting

The proposed section Hosting (docs/proposals/hosting-section.md, version 4): what runs on your machines and how it is reached, in one place. Its sections, in a list on the left as in Settings, are Applications (with containers, and the Catalogue below it), Models, Storage, Routes and Machines (R-428). The applications in one group for each machine are the manner of Coolify's servers (R-427); the page of one application with its Domains next to its General settings is the manner of Coolify; the Catalogue is a gallery with search and categories, as Arcane's Templates and Coolify's one-click services. Routes is the section that Settings holds today, moved, with the same frames as routes/index.html. Hosting sits directly below Code in the bar. A proposal with its direction decided: the requirements are not approved. The names, machines and numbers are examples.
